"Run your business. Not your WhatsApp."
One platform for small service businesses to manage their customers and their staff — the two things that make or break a small business every single day.

How They Merge Naturally
Think about a salon owner's daily reality:

Morning — check who's booked for today, which stylist is free at 2pm
Afternoon — a stylist shows up 2 hours late
End of month — manually calculate who worked how many days, who was late, who gets paid what

Your product solves all of that in one place. The appointment side and the payroll side are actually deeply connected because staff and bookings intersect — you assign bookings to specific staff, track their performance, and pay them based on work done.

Unified Feature Set
Customer Side (Booking)

Business gets a public booking page (yourbrand.slotsaff.com)
Customers book services, pick a staff member (e.g. "I want Amaka")
Auto WhatsApp/SMS reminders at 24hrs and 1hr before
Optional deposit to lock the booking
Owner sees full daily/weekly schedule in a calendar view
Staff Side (Attendance & Payroll)

Each staff member gets a clock-in link or PIN
System logs arrival time, flags lateness automatically
Bookings assigned to staff count toward their work record
End of month: one-click payroll calculation with deductions
Payslips sent via WhatsApp or email
The Bridge Between Both Sides This is the magic — when a staff member is assigned a booking, that booking automatically counts as a work session. You know they showed up because the customer was served. For commission-based staff (very common in Nigerian salons), the system can calculate pay as a percentage of services rendered. That's something no manual process can do cleanly.

Three Modules, One Dashboard
1. Bookings — your public-facing scheduling tool 2. Attendance — staff clock-in/out and work history 3. Payroll — auto-calculated, one-click, WhatsApp payslips

Every small business owner opens the app and sees: today's appointments, who's clocked in, and how much payroll is owed this month. That's your home dashboard — three numbers, total clarity.

Target Customers to Launch With
Salons and barbers are your beachhead. Here's why they're perfect:

They have staff (2–8 stylists)
They have appointments (walk-ins are dying, everyone messages first)
Staff are often commission-based — payroll is genuinely painful
Owners are active on Instagram — your booking link fits perfectly in their bio
They talk to each other — one salon owner tells five others

What to Build First
Don't build everything at once. Here's the sequence:

Month 1–2: Booking module only. Get 20 salons using it. Charge nothing yet. Month 3: Add attendance/clock-in. Existing users are already hooked on bookings. Month 4–5: Add payroll. Now you're indispensable. Month 6: Start charging. Conversion will be high because switching means losing all their data and history.

By the time you charge, they can't leave. That's the play.
